# OpenEval v3 asset inventory

All footage is privacy-reviewed dark-theme capture from the live local OpenEval dashboard. Each MP4 is 3.0 seconds, H.264, 1920x968, 30 fps, and silent. Matching stills use the same basename under `capture/assets/stills/`.

## Brand

OpenEval is a dark, data-dense operator console: black and elevated charcoal surfaces, violet as the primary accent, green reserved for confirmed positive evidence, and mono typography for metrics and provenance.
