OpenEval

One message: OpenEval turns scattered real-world agent activity into trustworthy operating evidence.

Audience: developers and operators who run agent CLIs and need to understand what happened, what changed, and whether an agent actually earned trust.

Proof surfaces available in the current product:
- 1.2k collected sessions across 10 sources
- live token, cache, model, tool, queue, and file-impact visibility
- data-quality provenance that distinguishes measured, inferred, missing, and malformed evidence
- configurable evaluation runs and a searchable case library
- side-by-side run comparison and harness leaderboard
- per-run confidence, deterministic proof, known-bad coverage, and weakness radar
- telemetry integrity and cost/performance views
- accuracy auditing with evidence-mix and case-quality diagnostics

Draft angle: from scattered runs to trustworthy operating evidence.
Language: English.
Destination: desktop embed / YouTube, 16:9.
Target length: approximately 42 seconds.
Narration: none in the v3 draft; kinetic copy, real product footage, local music, and restrained transition effects carry the story.
